## 2024-05-19 - Prevent unnecessary eager loading with raiseload('*')
**Learning:** Using `lazy="selectin"` in SQLAlchemy models for relationship loading defaults to eagerness, and FastAPI does not filter relationships proactively. This leads to heavy additional implicit DB queries for entities like `Product` during listing when those relationships aren't part of the response schema (e.g. `ProductOut` doesn't need `reviews`, `images`, etc.). Passing this at the service level avoids breaking other usages but solves it on the router endpoint.
**Action:** When querying lists with SQLAlchemy, use `.options(raiseload('*'))` rather than `noload(...)` to safely prevent eager loading of relationships when they are not explicitly required by the response schema. Pass load_options from the router instead of hardcoding it in the service level to maintain modularity.
