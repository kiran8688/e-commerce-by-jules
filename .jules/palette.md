## 2024-05-18 - [AppShell Navigation Icons Accessibility]
**Learning:** Native `title` attributes mapping to `aria-label` texts and clear `focus-visible` classes improve accessibility for icon-only buttons. The mobile menu toggle also needs an `aria-expanded` attribute.
**Action:** When creating icon-only buttons, always ensure they have informative `aria-label`, corresponding `title`, and `focus-visible` styles matching the brand identity. Keep toggle states accurate with `aria-expanded`.
