## 2024-05-29 - Dynamic Accessibility States and Tooltips for Icon-Only Controls
**Learning:** Icon-only controls in this application (like the Search, Account, and Cart buttons in AppShell) require native `title` attributes that mirror their `aria-labels` to provide tooltips for sighted users who may not understand the icon's meaning. Furthermore, toggle buttons (like the mobile menu) require dynamic `aria-expanded` attributes and dynamic text labels ("Open Menu" vs "Close Menu") to properly announce their state to screen readers.
**Action:** Always ensure that icon-only interactive elements have both `aria-label` and `title` attributes. For toggle buttons, bind `aria-expanded`, `aria-label`, and `title` to the state variable controlling the toggle to ensure accurate accessibility information is communicated at all times.
